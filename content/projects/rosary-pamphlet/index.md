A development prospecting inclusion and take-home devotional pamphlet that walks a visitor through the Holy Rosary — the four sets of mysteries with their scripture and fruits, the full set of prayers, and a plain method for praying it — illustrated throughout with the Basilica's own mosaic depictions of each mystery.

## Context

The Rosary's content is fixed and substantial: twenty mysteries across four sets, each with a scripture verse and a named fruit, plus the Apostles' Creed, Our Father, Hail Mary, and the concluding prayers. The design challenge is density without clutter — fitting all of it onto a few panels while keeping it legible, prayable, and beautiful enough to feel like a keepsake from the Shrine rather than a handout.

## Design

Aside from the photographic challenge of cataloging each instance of the Rosary's mysteries inside the Basilica and ensuring lighting and overall continuity, the pairing of each mystery with the Shrine's own artwork of that scene is the idea that makes this more than a generic Rosary card. The four mysteries panels, each with artwork, scripture reference, and the named fruit, carry the instructional weight. The prayer panels on the reverse hold the full texts. The result is a pamphlet a visitor can actually use while standing in the church, not just take home and set down.
