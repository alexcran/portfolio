A logo and identity mark for **RISE** — Maola's multi-year cooperative growth strategy built on four words: *Refine, Invest, Secure, Elevate*. The mark sets a bold "RISE" in a dotted-outline banner beneath an illustrated red barn and silo, flanked by grazing cows, with the Maola script wordmark and four-word tagline on a ribbon below.

**Second Place — Infographics, Illustration, and Graphic Art, 2025 NMPF Cooperative Communicators Contest.**

## Design Context

The mark had to feel unmistakably like Maola while standing on its own as a program identity. It draws directly on the cooperative's brand vocabulary and organizes it into a badge that reads at a glance and holds together at small sizes. The barn and cows do the warmth; the heavy banner type does the authority; the ribbon ties the program's four pillars to the name.
