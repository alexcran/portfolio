A very small selection of photographs from Holy Week and Easter at the Basilica of the National Shrine of the Immaculate Conception.

## Approach

Liturgical photography is a discipline of restraint. You work inside a sacred event without becoming part of it or detracting from it. You have to be able to find the image without a flash, without staging, without interrupting the rite.

The Shrine's own architecture and light do much of the work. The job is to be in the right place, quietly, when they come together.

The images were made to supply the Shrine's print, digital, and social communications, and to document these liturgies in a way that stood on its own as photography, not just documentation.
