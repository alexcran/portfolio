A selection of numerous promotional pieces for events at the Basilica of the National Shrine of the Immaculate Conception.

## Design

The Shrine offers an extraordinary visual vocabulary — mosaic, stained glass, the building itself — and these posters draw on it through original photographs and artwork made for each piece.


## My Role

Illustrator, photographer, and designer. The concert posters are intentionally more artistic. Across all three pieces I took the photographs, composed the layouts, set the typography, and prepared the files for print and digital distribution.
