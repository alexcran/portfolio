Publication design for *The Art of Accompaniment: Theological, Spiritual, and Practical Elements of Building a More Relational Church*, a Catholic Apostolate Center resource. Cover and complete interior typesetting for a book about accompaniment — the theological and spiritual work of walking alongside others — developed around the Emmaus image of companionship on the road.

The full book can be read online at the Catholic Apostolate Center.

## Context

Accompaniment is an interior, relational subject, and the design had to match that register — warm and restrained rather than corporate or busy. The cover leans on a single evocative painting of two figures walking together, with a spare type treatment that lets the image carry the feeling and signals the resource's seriousness without overdesigning it.

## My Role

I typeset and designed the entire book — cover and interior layout, the typographic system, and the full production from front matter through body. The text belongs to the named authors and developers; my work was giving it a visual form that honored the subject.
